How to Use Electrical Equipment Safely

These instructions are for students and workers who use electrical equipment at school, at home, or in the workplace. Follow them whenever you need to operate electrical equipment safely.

What you need

  • Appropriate PPE, such as safety gloves and protective footwear.
  • Electrical equipment in good condition.
  • A dry working area with enough lighting.
  • Basic safety instructions from a qualified person.

Steps

  1. Inspect the equipment and its power cable before use.
  2. Make sure your hands and the working area are dry.
  3. Read the manufacturer's instructions before operating the equipment.
  4. Use the equipment only for its intended purpose.
  5. Never use damaged electrical equipment, because it can cause electric shock, burns, or fire.
  6. Switch off and disconnect the equipment after use, if safe to do so.

If something goes wrong

If you notice smoke, sparks, a burning smell, or a damaged cable, stop using the equipment immediately. Do not touch exposed wires. If it is safe, switch off the power and keep others away. Report the problem to a qualified electrician. Do not attempt electrical repairs unless you are trained and authorised.

Always record any faults you find so that they can be reported and corrected.

Electrical power may be measured in kVA.
