How to Apply for a Student ID Card

Applying for a student ID card helps you identify yourself as a registered student and access student services.

Before You Go

Check the location and opening hours of the student affairs office before visiting.

What to Bring

Prepare the documents and information required to complete your application.

Identification Documents

Bring your student registration number and an accepted form of identification.

Photographs and Forms

Prepare a passport-size photograph and any application forms requested by your institution.

At the Office

Visit the student affairs office and ask for the student ID application form.

After Applying

Ask when your card will be ready and follow the instructions for collecting it.
